Hi, I'm Tumim

I am from Ethiopia, and I attend a private medical college. I live in a country where opportunities are not simply handed to you. Here, attending a private college often means your family has made significant sacrifices.

So I started becoming faithful with whatever was placed in my hands. I started this website to remind myself that even though I was not born into opportunity, I am determined to create one through discipline, integrity, and faith.

On this website, I won't hand you shortcuts or present a long list of opportunities. I am here to encourage you to start with what you already have, if you are willing to be faithful with it.

Mission

What I Value

Slow, Careful Steps Over Haste

Progress built on solid foundations lasts longer than quick wins. I believe in intentional, thoughtful growth—even if it means moving slower than others. Depth matters more than speed.

Discipline Over Motivation

Motivation fades, but discipline remains. I commit to showing up consistently, regardless of how I feel. Small daily actions compound into meaningful transformation over time.

Faith Over Fear

Fear can paralyze, but faith moves us forward. I choose to trust in something greater than my circumstances, knowing that with God at the center, everything works together for good.
