About Me

Hi,
I'm Tumim.

From Ethiopia. Medical student. Committed to building something meaningful with whatever I've been given.

My Story

I am from Ethiopia, and I attend a private medical college. I live in a country where opportunities are not simply handed to you. Here, attending a private college often means your family has made significant sacrifices. So I started becoming faithful with whatever was placed in my hands — and I built this space to prove that you don't need to be born into opportunity to create one.

Mission

A space for thoughtful growth, disciplined living, and God-centered transformation. I won't hand you shortcuts. I am here to encourage you to start with what you already have — if you are willing to be faithful with it.

Vision

For people determined to grow beyond their circumstances — through discipline, integrity, and faith.

What I Value

Principles I Live By

01

Slow, Careful Steps Over Haste

Progress built on solid foundations lasts longer than quick wins. I believe in intentional, thoughtful growth — even if it means moving slower than others. Depth matters more than speed.

02

Discipline Over Motivation

Motivation fades, but discipline remains. I commit to showing up consistently, regardless of how I feel. Small daily actions compound into meaningful transformation over time.

03

Faith Over Fear

Fear can paralyze, but faith moves us forward. I choose to trust in something greater than my circumstances, knowing that with God at the center, everything works together for good.
